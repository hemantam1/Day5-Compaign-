1. ADVERTISING STRUCTURE (PPC)
Automatic Discovery Campaigns: Utilizing Amazon’s internal AI to identify which search terms naturally convert for our specific products.

Manual Bidding Optimization:

Top of Search (TOS) Bidding: Increasing bids by 50-100% for high-intent keywords to secure the #1 spot.

Negative Keyword Filtering: Using AI to instantly block irrelevant searches (e.g., "Aloe Vera Hair Gel") to save ad spend.

Competitor Targeting: Placing Sponsored Display ads directly on competitor product pages to capture their traffic.

2. AI SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (Automation Workflow)
The proposed system will function on the following technology logic:

Data Collection: Fetching real-time Amazon Search Term Reports and Business Reports.

Decision Logic (AI Agent):

High Performance: If a keyword has a high conversion rate, the AI automatically increases the bid.

Low Performance: If a keyword spends budget without sales, the AI pauses or lowers the bid.

Execution: Leveraging Amazon’s Selling Partner API (SP-API) to push these changes automatically without manual intervention.

3. CONVERSION & VISUAL OPTIMIZATION
AI-Generated Creative: Using AI image tools to create lifestyle backgrounds (e.g., a fresh kitchen setting) to make the product look premium.

Video Ads: Implementing 15-second AI-generated video loops that showcase the "Immunity" and "Detox" benefits, which occupy more mobile screen space.
